## 1. How to batch-export with the 🎬 dialog (PR #13)

For **every row** in the lesson tables below:

1. Open **Backing Tracks** → bottom tab **Templates** (📄) → tab **🎓 Lessons / שיעורים**.
2. Press the lesson number. Each row is one preset; under it are its **🎬 video versions** (chips). Press the chip of the version to export (the first one is the default; **Load / טען** = default). This fills style, chords, tempo, key, the live neck view **and the 🎬 export settings** for that version. It never starts playback.
3. Press **🎬** (info strip). The dialog is already filled: *Repetitions*, *Change neck view every*, *Necks on screen* (1 or 2) and the *Neck views* list (per view: shape or position combo, shape key, scale + root, pentatonic highlight, display "Note names").
4. *Video size*: **720p** (≈ 2.5 Mbit/s video + 160 kbit/s audio → ≈ 160 MB for 8 min). Press **Start export**, keep the tab in front, then **Download**. Identical rows in different lessons (same track + neck + version) are the same video — export once (see the unique-video count in §5).

Duration math (shown per row): *rep* = beats × 60 / BPM; repetitions = round(480 s / rep) nudged into [300, 585] s so that count-in (style intro) + ending (≈ 15 s allowance) still land inside 5–10 min; neck view changes every round(60 s / rep) repetitions (≈ once a minute). "Dialog" = the dialog's own estimate (adds a 1-bar count-in).

### Two-neck video layout (Mario, answers 2 and 8)

*Necks on screen = 2* shows two aligned necks (same fret range, same fret at the same x). The chord header is compacted to one row (current chord large, next chord + beat dots on the right) and the chord strip stays, so it is always clear which chord plays; the logo/side panel is unchanged.

- **All CAGED positions**: neck 1 = C · G · D, neck 2 = A · E. These windows never overlap (C 0–3, G 4–8, D 9–13 / A 2–6, E 7–10 in C; shifted per key, the second neck uses the octave closest to the first).
- **Octave positions**: neck 1 = 1-3-4, neck 2 = 1-2-4 (their union is the hybrid, which is also its own single-neck video).
- Each position is outlined by a coloured, labelled frame (C orange, A yellow, G green, E blue, D purple; octave boxes one frame per string pair). Dots, roots and chord tones keep their usual colours.
- *Full scale + pentatonic* (answer 6): every note of the full scale is shown; notes outside the pentatonic are drawn smaller and fainter (extra legend row).
- **Rule (round 3): the full working scale is always visible** — in the video and in the live neck. Arpeggio lessons (5–8) highlight the arpeggio on top (other scale notes smaller/fainter, legend "Other scale notes"); blues lessons highlight the pentatonic on top; the other necks colour the chord tones over the normal scale. A view without a scale uses the track key's scale, so a chord-tones-only or pentatonic-only neck cannot occur (unit- and e2e-tested).
